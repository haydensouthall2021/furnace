How it works

There is no tax. There is a furnace.

Most burn tokens take a cut of your trade. This one doesn't touch your trade at all.

✓Your trade is untouched

No transfer tax, no buy tax, no sell tax. You pay exactly what you'd pay on any other token. Nothing is deducted and no wallet is exempt — because there is nothing to be exempt from.

✓The fuel is creator fees

The launchpad already pays a creator fee on every trade. Instead of sitting in a wallet, it's swept out, spent buying this token on the open market, and the tokens are destroyed.

✓Burned means burned

A real burn instruction against the mint. Not sent to a dead address, not parked in a treasury. Removed from total supply, irreversibly.

✓Volume makes it faster

More trading means more fees means more buying and burning. The number above falls faster the busier it gets. That's the whole design.
